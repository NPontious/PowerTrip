What did that drive actually cost?

PowerTrip reads your car's own sensors and tells you what each drive cost, in gasoline and electricity, to the cent. Every number is measured by the car or worked out from your receipts. Nothing is modeled or estimated.

See the screens Read the spec (PDF)

Only what was measured

Battery power, fuel flow, speed and levels come straight from the car. No "saved vs. a gas car", no air-drag guesses, no expected MPGe.

Costs from your receipts

Each fill-up and charge is priced from what you paid, and used oldest first. A free charge costs exactly $0, and drive totals add up to the cent.

Compared with your typical

A drive is judged against the median of your car's other drives, not against a sticker number. No scores, no scolding.

Four numbers on every trip

In the same order on every car. When one doesn't apply, the screen says so instead of hiding it.

  • Cost per mileFrom your receipts
  • Efficiencymi/kWh, mpg, or both
  • Fuel-freeShare of moving miles with no fuel burning
  • RegenShare of the battery's output put back
How it works

No phone in the loop

A small adapter in the car's OBD port does the logging, and your own server does the math.

  1. The adapter logs while you drive

    A WiCAN Pro in the OBD-II port records the car's signals to its own memory. It needs no phone and no cell plan, and sleeps when the car is off.

  2. It syncs when you get home

    Parked on your Wi-Fi, it uploads the drive to your home server in a few seconds.

  3. Your server prices the drive

    The server works out energy and cost per drive. The Android app and the web page only show what it calculated.

Your data stays on your server. There's no PowerTrip cloud, and the app never asks for your phone's location. Position comes only from the car or the adapter.
