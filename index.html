Core Architectural Principles

Engineered for Physical Reality

Commercial OBD apps and OEM cloud subscriptions fail vehicle owners with synthetic estimates, cloud lock-in, and alarmist fault codes. PowerTrip replaces them with physics and math.

Energy Lot Ledger

Discrete First-In First-Out (FIFO) fuel and electricity accounting. Tracks overnight home charging at your utility rate ($0.220 / kWh) and gasoline per fill-up receipt. Solves PHEV dual-fuel splitting without synthetic blends.

Pending receipt reconciliation Automatic Prompts

Only What Was Measured

Every value on screen is read from the vehicle CAN-bus (battery pack current, mass airflow, fuel flow, vehicle speed) or follows by simple arithmetic. No modeled aerodynamic drag, no expected MPGe, and no theoretical "saved vs. gas car" claims.

EPA road-load verification Zero Synthetic Drag

Owner-First DTC Diagnostics

Translates raw Diagnostic Trouble Codes into plain-English fault descriptions and actionable safe-to-drive guidance. Codes are linked to the exact trip and engine conditions when triggered. Zero premature code clearing protects emissions readiness.

Non-alarming severity rank Safe-to-Drive Status

Telematics Vocabulary & Invariants

Standardized terminology: each measurement has exactly one meaning across EV, PHEV, HEV, and Gas vehicles.

Invariant Metric

Fuel-free

Moving with no fuel burning (speed > 0.5 mph and fuel flow ≤ 0.05 gal/h). True across EV (100%), PHEV (electric miles), HEV (EV glide), and Gas (coasting fuel cut).

Replaces “EV Mode”
Kinetic Recovery

Regen

High-voltage battery charging while no fuel burns (battery power < 0 with fuel flow ≤ 0.05 gal/h). Expressed consistently in Wh per mile across all electrified cars.

In Wh / mi (not %)
Idle & Auxiliary

Stopped

Vehicle speed below 0.5 mph. Energy consumed while stopped (HVAC heating/cooling, electronics, engine idle) is isolated so moving efficiency remains unskewed.

Speed < 0.5 mph
Cost Model

Energy Lot Ledger

Discrete FIFO queue tracking exact purchase costs of electricity and gasoline batches. Prevents artificial averaging across home charging and public fast-charging.

Exact FIFO Batches
