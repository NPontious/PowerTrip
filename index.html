PowerTrip: Fuel & Energy Tracker

A cross-platform mobile application built with Flutter to track vehicle fuel, energy usage, and associated costs.

View Features

Features in Action

Dashboard at a Glance

The home screen provides an immediate overview of your vehicle's status, including current electric and gas fuel levels, and a list of your most recent trips.

Detailed Expense Tracking

Monitor your fuel expenses with an interactive chart and a detailed transaction history. Easily add new entries for gas or electric charging and filter by transaction type.

Visual Fuel Gauges

Get a technical-style dashboard view of your gas and electric levels. The gauges are sectioned to show individual fill-ups or charges, providing a clear picture of your remaining fuel.

Comprehensive Trip Analysis

Drill down into individual trips to see a full breakdown of miles driven, total cost, and the specific amounts of gas and electricity consumed, helping you understand the efficiency of each journey.
