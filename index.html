PowerTrip: Smart Fuel & Energy Tracking

A cross-platform mobile app to track vehicle fuel, energy usage, and associated costs, giving you a clear picture of your spending.

See Features

How It Works

1. Track Your Trips

Automatically log distance, cost, and fuel consumption for every journey.

2. Analyze Your Data

View detailed breakdowns and charts to understand your spending patterns.

3. Save Money

Optimize your fuel and energy usage by making data-driven decisions.

Features in Action

Detailed Expense Tracking

Monitor fuel expenses with an interactive chart and a detailed transaction history. Easily add new entries and filter by type.

Visual Fuel Gauges

Get a technical dashboard view of your gas and electric levels, with breakdowns for each fill-up or charge.

Comprehensive Trip Analysis

Drill down into individual trips to see a full breakdown of miles, cost, and fuel consumed to understand the efficiency of each journey.

Customizable Settings

Configure server details, manage saved places, and customize the app to fit your needs.
